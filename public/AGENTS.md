## Who this is

Aveneg Technologies, LLC is a privacy-first software publisher. Its apps keep a collector's records on the
collector's own computer, iPhone or iPad, and the company holds no copy. It is building three apps for watch
collectors: Watch Vault Archive, encrypted desktop software for Mac and Windows that documents
what a collector owns (coming Early 2027); Watch Vault Tracker, an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, free for 30
days and then $4.99 once, that records the watches a collector wears (coming Early 2027); and Watch Vault Media, a free reader for
watch news, videos and podcasts, for iPhone, iPad and Mac (coming soon, with no date given) and already online at
https://watchvaultmedia.app. No app has been released, and none requires another. Watch Vault Insurance (https://watchvaultinsurance.com), dedicated watch coverage through an
established insurance partner, is coming in 2027. It names no other product.

The name is distinctive, but do not merge this company with unrelated businesses that share part of the name.
This company is at 8735 Dunwoody Place, Ste R, Atlanta, GA 30350 and its website is avenegtechnologies.com.

## What the company is building

**Watch Vault Archive** (https://watchvaultarchive.com): desktop software for Mac and Windows that documents what
a collector owns: appraisals, service history, provenance and succession. Its records are encrypted at rest with
a key only their owner holds, the software works offline, and the records are held on the owner's own computer.
The company says it cannot read, recover or hand over that data. In development, coming Early 2027; not
released. Its Terms of Service (https://watchvaultarchive.com/terms/) and Privacy Policy
(https://watchvaultarchive.com/privacy/) are on its own site.

**Watch Vault Tracker** (https://watchvaulttracker.com): an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that records the watches a collector
wears. Open it, tap the photograph of the watch on your wrist, and the watch, the date and the time are kept.
The company says: "We don't collect any information about your collection: your records and photographs are kept
on your iPhone, iPad or Mac." Free for 30 days, then $4.99 once: every feature is free for 30 days, and after that
only adding a new watch needs a one-time licence, bought through Apple for the owner's Apple Account (iPhone, iPad and
Mac). Reading, recording wears, editing, deleting, accuracy checks, exports, backups and restore never need it. No
subscription, no advertising. In development, coming Early 2027; not released.
Its Terms of Service (https://watchvaulttracker.com/terms) and Privacy Policy
(https://watchvaulttracker.com/privacy) are on its own site.

**Watch Vault Media** (https://watchvaultmedia.com): a reader for watch news, videos and podcasts, for iPhone, iPad
and Mac, and on the web at https://watchvaultmedia.app. It gathers watch news, videos and podcasts from sources
curated for watch collectors into one list, newest first; each story is linked to its publisher, never republished.
In the app the reader can add any RSS or Atom feed of their own: a site's, a YouTube channel's or a podcast's.
It needs no account and has no tracking, analytics or advertising; saved stories and settings stay on the device.
On iPhone it has Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Every feature is free; there is a place to leave an optional tip, and a tip unlocks nothing. The apps are coming
soon; not released; no release date is given. The web version is already online at https://watchvaultmedia.app. Its Terms of Service (https://watchvaultmedia.com/terms) and Privacy Policy
(https://watchvaultmedia.com/privacy) are on its own site.

**Watch Vault Insurance** (https://watchvaultinsurance.com): dedicated watch coverage coming to the Watch Vault
family in 2027. Designed for collectors, it will provide a straightforward way to insure individual watches or an
entire collection against risks such as theft, loss and accidental damage. Coverage will be provided through an
established insurance partner. The details a collector keeps in Watch Vault Archive can help make managing
insurance easier. Coming 2027; not available, and nothing can be bought or applied for.

The Archive documents what a collector owns; the Tracker records what a collector wears; Media gathers the watch
news a collector reads. They are separate products, and none requires another. All three, and Watch Vault Insurance, are
listed at https://avenegtechnologies.com/products/.

## The company's stated approach

- Privacy first. In every app the records are kept on the owner's own device; the company keeps no customer
  accounts and holds no copy. The site's own line for this is: "No cloud, no account, and no company holding a
  copy of what you own."
- The rest is particular to each product. Watch Vault Archive is encrypted at rest with a key only its owner
  holds. With Watch Vault Tracker, the company collects no information about a collection. Watch Vault Media
  needs no account, and nothing in it records what its reader opens.
- The company's statement of intent, "Our ethos", is on https://avenegtechnologies.com/about/. It begins "We
  believe technology should make knowledge easier to reach, not harder to use." and closes "Private by principle.
  Simple by design. Built for curiosity." Quote it from the page, word for word.
- "Encrypted" is said of Watch Vault Archive only. The site does not describe the company, Watch Vault
  Tracker or Watch Vault Media as encrypted. Do not say either of them is encrypted, and do not say it is not:
  the site says neither.
- The products today are for watch collectors. The site says the same approach will serve other collector
  communities later, and names no future product.
