What we publish

Four products, each with its own site.

Watch VaultTM Archive

In development, · coming Early 2027

Documents the watches a collector owns.

Desktop software for a collection’s appraisals, service history, provenance and succession. Aveneg cannot read, recover or hand over what you record in it.

For
Mac and Windows
Visit watchvaultarchive.com

Watch Vault Tracker

In development, · coming Early 2027

Records the watches a collector wears.

Open it, tap the photograph of the watch on your wrist, and the watch, the date and the time are kept. Free for 30 days, then $4.99 once: after that, only adding a new watch needs the licence. No subscription, no advertising.

For
iPhone, iPad and Mac
Visit watchvaulttracker.com

Watch Vault Media

Coming soon

Gathers the watch news a collector reads.

Watch news, videos and podcasts from sources curated for watch collectors, in one list, newest first. In the app you can add any RSS or Atom feed of your own. Each story opens where its publisher put it. Every feature is free: no account, no advertising, no tracking.

For
iPhone, iPad and Mac
Web
Already online at watchvaultmedia.app
Visit watchvaultmedia.com

Watch VaultTM Insurance

Coming 2027

Coverage for the watches you care about.

A straightforward way to insure individual watches or an entire collection against risks such as theft, loss and accidental damage. The details you keep in Watch Vault Archive can help make managing insurance easier.
