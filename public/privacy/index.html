Privacy Policy

Last updated September 20, 2026

This policy covers avenegtechnologies.com only — the corporate website you are reading now. It does not cover any software the Company publishes. Each product has its own, separate Privacy Policy, linked from its entry on the Products page, that describes how that product handles data.

The short version: this site collects nothing about you automatically. No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts. If you never email us, we have no data about you at all.

1. What we collect from this site

Nothing, by design. This site is static — there are no accounts, no forms, no cookies, and no analytics or tracking scripts of any kind running on it.

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, generates standard web server logs as an ordinary part of serving any website (for example, IP address and request timing, for security and reliability purposes). We do not access or analyze these logs as a matter of routine, and they are not used to build a profile of you.

2. If you email us

If you write to info@avenegtechnologies.com or legal@avenegtechnologies.com, we receive your email address and whatever you send. We use it only to respond to you. We do not add you to a mailing list, sell it, or share it with anyone, other than the infrastructure that delivers the email to us in the first place (see below).

3. Third parties

This site is hosted and its email is routed by Cloudflare, Inc., which operates the infrastructure that serves these pages and delivers mail sent to our addresses. Cloudflare’s own privacy policy governs its handling of data in that role.

4. Children

This site is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

5. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the version currently in effect.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy: legal@avenegtechnologies.com
