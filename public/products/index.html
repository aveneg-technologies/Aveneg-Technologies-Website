What we publish.

Aveneg Technologies is building two products for watch collectors. They are separate products, and neither requires the other.

Watch Vault Archive™

In development, · coming Winter 2027

Watch Vault Archive is desktop software that documents what a collector owns.

It is built for record-keeping over the long term. Its records are encrypted at rest with a key only their owner holds, and the software works offline. Aveneg Technologies cannot read, recover or hand over that data.

For
Mac and Windows
Records
Appraisals, service history, provenance and succession
Kept
On your own computer
Privacy
No account with us. No copy held by Aveneg.
Visit watchvaultarchive.com

In development, · coming Early 2027

Watch Vault Tracker is an iPhone and iPad app that records the watches a collector wears.

Open it, tap the photograph of the watch on your wrist, and the watch, the date and the time are kept. We don’t collect any information about your collection: your records and photographs are kept on your iPhone or iPad. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no advertising and no trial; there is a place to leave an optional tip, and a tip unlocks nothing.

For
iPhone and iPad
Records
Each watch worn, with its date and time
Kept
On your iPhone or iPad
Privacy

Cost
Free for watch collectors
