About

A software company built around one idea.

The idea is that a record of something valuable stays where its owner put it, and nowhere else.

Our ethos

We believe technology should make knowledge easier to reach, not harder to use.

We respect the privacy, independence, and opinions of every person who uses our products. We design to reduce friction, remove unnecessary complexity, and minimize the mental fatigue that too often comes with technology.

Our goal is simple: create thoughtful tools that help people explore, understand, preserve, and enjoy the subjects they care about.

For Watch Vault, that means making the world of horology more approachable without making it shallow — welcoming newcomers while respecting experienced collectors, and helping preserve the knowledge, stories, and enthusiasm of watch collecting for future generations.

Private by principle. Simple by design. Built for curiosity.

Why

A record of something valuable is worth protecting like the thing itself. Held on a company’s server, it can be exposed in ways its owner never sees: a breach at the provider, a demand served on the company instead of the person, a service that closes and takes years of entries with it. Software that keeps the record on the owner’s own device removes those risks by removing the server.

How we build

Privacy comes first. In every app the records are kept on the owner’s own device; the company keeps no customer accounts and holds no copy. The rest is particular to each product. Watch Vault Archive is encrypted at rest with a key only its owner holds, so the company cannot read, recover or hand over that data. With Watch Vault Tracker, the company collects no information about a collection. Watch Vault Media needs no account, and nothing in it records what you read.

What we publish

The company is building three apps for watch collectors. Watch Vault Archive documents what a collector owns; Watch Vault Tracker records what a collector wears; Watch Vault Media gathers the watch news a collector reads. They are separate products, and none requires another. Watch Vault Insurance, coming in 2027, will bring dedicated watch coverage to the family, provided through an established insurance partner.
