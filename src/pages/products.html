Watch Vault Archive is desktop software that documents what a collector owns.

It is built for record-keeping over the long term. Its records are encrypted at rest with a key only their owner holds, and the software works offline. Aveneg Technologies cannot read, recover or hand over that data.

Watch Vault Tracker is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that records the watches a collector wears.

Open it, tap the photograph of the watch on your wrist, and the watch, the date and the time are kept. We don’t collect any information about your collection: your records and photographs are kept on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Every feature is free, with no subscription, no advertising and no trial; there is a place to leave an optional tip, and a tip unlocks nothing.

Watch Vault Media is a reader for watch news and video, for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and on the web.

It gathers the latest stories and videos from sites and channels chosen for watch collectors into one list, newest first. Each story opens where its publisher put it: linked, never republished. It needs no account and has no tracking, analytics or advertising; your saved stories and settings stay on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. On iPhone, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the newest stories. Every feature is free; there is a place to leave an optional tip, and a tip unlocks nothing.
