Watch Vault Archive is desktop software that documents what a collector owns.

It is built for record-keeping over the long term. Its records are encrypted at rest with a key only their owner holds, and the software works offline. Copies leave the computer only as files you save, including encrypted backups in a folder you pick. Aveneg Technologies cannot read, recover or hand over that data.

Watch Vault Tracker is an app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that records the watches a collector wears.

Open it, tap the photograph of the watch on your wrist, and the watch, the date and the time are kept. We don’t collect any information about your collection: your records and photographs are kept on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and in your own iCloud if you turn on Sync across devices. If your device backs up to iCloud, Apple’s backup includes them. Every feature is free for 30 days, then $4.99 once: after that, only adding a new watch needs a one-time licence, bought through Apple for your Apple Account. Recording wears, editing, accuracy checks, exports and backups never need it. No subscription, no advertising.

Watch Vault Media is a reader for watch news, videos and podcasts, for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is also on the web.

It gathers watch news, videos and podcasts from sources curated for watch collectors into one list, newest first. In the app you can add any RSS or Atom feed of your own: a site’s, a YouTube channel’s or a podcast’s. Each story opens where its publisher put it: linked, never republished. It needs no account and adds no tracking, analytics or advertising of its own; a publisher’s page shows its own. Your saved stories and settings are kept on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and in your own iCloud if you turn on Sync across devices. If your device is backed up, Apple’s backup may include them. On iPhone, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show the newest stories. Every feature is free; there is a place to leave an optional tip, and a tip unlocks nothing.

Watch Vault Insurance will bring dedicated watch coverage to the Watch Vault family.

Designed for collectors, it will provide a straightforward way to insure individual watches or an entire collection against risks such as theft, loss and accidental damage. Coverage will be provided through an established insurance partner, while Watch Vault gives collectors a simpler way to document their watches and keep the information needed for insurance in one place. If you already use Watch Vault Archive, the details you keep there can help make managing insurance easier.

Watch Vault History is a free reference website on the history of watchmaking.

It tells the history of watchmaking as one connected story, in five eras: through the companies, people, places, technologies, objects and events it is made of, each written from cited sources. It is a website, with no app.
